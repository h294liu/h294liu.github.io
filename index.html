Our research advances hydrologic modeling and forecasting, with a focus on extreme events such as floods and droughts.

Key research questions include:

  • What drives extreme hydrologic events?
  • Which hydrologic processes dominate across different regions and scales?
  • How can watershed representation and parameter estimation be improved in hydrologic models?
  • How can observational uncertainties be quantified and incorporated into modeling and forecasting?
  • How can predictions of floods, droughts, and other hydrologic extremes be improved?

Our research combines in situ observations, remote sensing, process-based hydrologic modeling, and machine learning. We conduct large-domain simulations on high-performance computing systems and are committed to open, reproducible, and collaborative research. Our work aims to improve hydrologic prediction and support water resources engineering and environmental decision-making.
